The timing tradeoff

03 / UNDER THE HOOD

Continuity is the start.
Naturalness is the next test.

The Director reads the outgoing endpoint, measures position and velocity, aligns the incoming motion in the target’s space, and creates a separate reversible transition action.

Its quintic position and quaternion-log bridge is kinematic interpolation. It does not understand weight shifts, enforce balance, solve full-body IK, or guarantee believable choreography.

Read the engine design ↗

What this demonstration establishes

Actual engine output. The Director panel uses the sequence produced by the reviewed executor. The controls use actual Blender Actions and NLA, not a JavaScript recreation of blending.

Identical presentation. Every view uses the same locked camera, lighting, joint geometry and elapsed clock. Only the animation composition changes.

An honest stronger baseline. Aligned NLA may also look good. This benchmark does not claim the Director wins for every pose pair.

Limited motion. The generated rig glides and moves its arms; it is not a skilled dancer. Smooth sampled boundaries are not proof of natural human performance.

No private assets. No Adventurer, private Mixamo file, user configuration or production scene is published.
